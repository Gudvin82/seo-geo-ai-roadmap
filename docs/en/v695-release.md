# v6.9.5 Release Summary

`v6.9.5` adds an original, approval-first Content Operations Engine.

## What Changed

- Added a content operations queue that turns operator-supplied demand into
  explainable, owned editorial tasks.
- Added mandatory factual, editorial, legal, technical, and post-publish gates.
- Added a bilingual operating playbook for demand, briefs, quality controls,
  freshness, and cannibalization review.
- Kept automated publication explicitly out of scope.

## Boundary

The queue is a planning aid. Its score is not a forecast of rankings, traffic,
conversions, or AI citations.
