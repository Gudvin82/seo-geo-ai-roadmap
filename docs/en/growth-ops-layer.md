# Growth Ops Layer

`v6.9.1` adds a practical growth layer on top of the repository's audit and
proof foundation.

This layer is about turning findings into operator work:

- conversion ops for money pages and lead flows
- content architecture ops for page systems and schema coverage
- customer-research packs for messaging and comparison surfaces
- launch ops for honest public posts, release notes, and showcase routing

Primary scripts:

- `scripts/conversion_ops_pack.py`
- `scripts/content_growth_ops.py`
- `scripts/customer_research_pack.py`
- `scripts/launch_ops_pack.py`

Primary app surfaces:

- `conversion-ops-center`
- `content-growth-center`
- `research-ops-center`
- `launch-ops-center`

Operator rule:

- do not stop at diagnosis
- route every serious finding into a page, owner, proof input, and launch-safe
  explanation
