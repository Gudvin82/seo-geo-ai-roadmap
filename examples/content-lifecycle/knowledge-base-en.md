# Content Knowledge Base

## Article Standard

- One primary intent and one accountable owner per page.
- Material factual claims have reviewable sources.
- The page provides a direct answer before supporting detail.
- Internal links have a user purpose and intentional destination.
- Applicable structured data describes visible page content.

## Stop List

- Invented facts, testimonials, prices, credentials, or results
- Rewritten competitor copy
- Word-count targets used as a quality proxy
- Hidden text or deceptive search-only blocks
- Automatic publication without explicit human approval

## Definition Of Done

The factual, technical, editorial/legal, and post-publish gates are recorded as
evidence. Rankings, traffic, conversions, and citations remain observed outcomes,
not promised results.
